# Wanderer Mail

Wanderer at the mailbox. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- Wanderer's mailbox, in place of the game's window: a heading that says what waits (the letters, the gold to take, what is to pay on delivery, the next letter leaving, your free slots); every letter in one list, grouped (the auction house's, the players', the others), each group with its own Take all; the letter chosen read in place (its words, the auction house's account, its gold and items as squares: a click takes one, Shift links it, Ctrl tries it on), with Take, Pay and take (asked first, the amount said), Reply, Send it back (asked first), Delete (only where the game allows it, asked first while it still holds something), Report (in the game's window, where its button is) and Keep a copy.
- Write, in the same window: the name completed as you type, your characters, the players you wrote to lately, your friends and your guild online one click away; the subject, the message (Enter starts a new line, never sends), twelve squares for items (dropped on them, or right-clicked in your bags), gold sent or cash on delivery (never more than you have), the postage; Send asks first when gold leaves or is asked.
- The game's own mailbox is always one click away (Game window), every feature of it there; a link above it comes back. Escape ends the visit as the game's window does. With Postal or TradeSkillMaster's mailing, the game's window stays the one used until you choose otherwise (the setting "Wanderer's mailbox").
- With Wanderer's mailbox off, the mailbox card, beside the game's window: the letters, the gold inside, those to pay on delivery, the free slots of your bags, the first letter about to go back to its sender; then the auction house's letters (sold, back to you, won, outbid) and the players' letters, each block with its own Take these.
- Take the gold: only the gold of the letters, everything else stays. Take all only where the game has no such button of its own. Letters to pay on delivery are never taken.
- While it takes: Stop 12 / 40 on the button, a click stops it; afterwards the card says what was taken, with one line in the chat.
- Bags full: an item joins a stack of its own kind when it can, one free slot is kept, the rest stays in its letter and the take goes on with the gold.
- Beyond 50 letters: once the server brings the next ones, the card offers to go on (a click).
- On each letter: what happens when its days are over (back to its sender, or destroyed); an option takes those leaving soonest first.
- Shift+click on a letter takes it, Ctrl+click sends a player's letter back after a question.
- Send to: a small arrow at the end of the recipient field opens a list of your other characters of this realm and side (class color, level), the players you wrote to lately, your friends and your guild online. One click fills the recipient.
- A quiet word in the recipient field when gold or items go to a name you do not know, or when one of your own characters is asked to pay on delivery.
- Your other characters' letters with gold or items leaving within three days: a quiet notice at login, once a day, and their line in Wanderer's characters window.

Nothing is ever taken, paid, sent, sent back or deleted by itself: always on a click, asked first where gold leaves. The take buttons step aside for Postal or TradeSkillMaster's mailing. Settings in Wanderer's options; /wmail says whether a letter waits for you; /wmail dump, at a mailbox, writes down each letter's sender, subject and flags in the saved settings.
