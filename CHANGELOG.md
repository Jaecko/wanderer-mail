# Wanderer Mail 1.3.10

### New
- Wanderer's own mailbox: every letter in one list, grouped (auction house, players, others), each group with its own Take all; letters from a game master are left for you to take one by one, as in the game.
- Read a letter in place: its words, the auction house's account laid out, its gold and items as squares (a click takes, Shift links, Ctrl tries on).
- Pay on delivery, Reply, Send it back, Delete and Keep a copy beside the letter; paying, sending back and deleting a letter that still holds something always ask first.
- Write in the same window: names completed as you type, your characters, friends and guild one click away, twelve squares for items (drop them, or right click in your bags), gold or cash on delivery, the postage; asked first before any gold leaves.
- The game's own mailbox is always one click away (Game window), and a link brings Wanderer's back. With Postal or TradeSkillMaster, the game's window stays the default; the option Wanderer's mailbox, off, keeps the game's window with the card beside it.
- Walking away from the mailbox closes Wanderer's window with the game's, as soon as the mailbox is out of reach.

### Changed
- The recipients button shows a plain down arrow, as the other lists do.
- A letter holding gold or items about to go back or be lost is said in red.
- The recipients list says how many more names it could not show; its tooltip names your friends and guild members online.
- /wmail also tells what the mailbox card does.

### Fixed
- A stack put in a letter, or in a letter received, shows its count again (it was drawn under the item's icon), and the square lights under the mouse.
- With a controller, the mailbox window takes the controller as it opens (it waited to be chosen).
