# Wanderer Mail 1.3.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (needs Wanderer 1.4 or later). It sits beside the game's own mail window and replaces nothing of it. It never deletes, sends or pays anything: every take starts from your click, and a letter to pay on delivery is never taken.

### New
- The mailbox card: beside the mailbox, the letters, the gold inside, those to pay on delivery, the free slots of your bags and the first letter about to go back to its sender.
- The auction house's letters in their own block (sold, back to you, won, outbid, with counts and gold), the players' letters in another, each with its own Take these.
- Take the gold: only the gold of the letters; their items, and the letters to pay on delivery, stay. Take all only where the game has no such button of its own.
- While it takes, the button says how far (Stop 12 / 40) and stops at a click; afterwards the card says what was taken, with one line in the chat.
- Bags full: an item joins a stack of its own kind when it can, one free slot is kept, the rest waits in its letter and the take goes on with the gold.
- Beyond 50 letters: the card says how many more the server keeps, and once it brings them, one click goes on with the next letters.
- On each letter of the inbox, what happens when its days are over: back to its sender, or destroyed; warm when it holds gold or items and leaves soon.
- An option (off) takes the letters leaving soonest first.
- Shift+click on a letter takes it; Ctrl+click on a player's letter sends it back, after a question.
- Send to: a small arrow at the end of the recipient field lists your other characters of this realm and side (class color, level), the players you wrote to lately, your friends and your guild online; one click fills the recipient.
- A quiet word in the recipient field when gold or items go to a name you do not know, or when one of your own characters is asked to pay on delivery.
- Your other characters' letters with gold or items leaving within three days: a quiet notice at login, once a day, and their line in Wanderer's characters window. Your own letter going back within a day joins Wanderer's reminders; the town card says it too.
- With Postal or TradeSkillMaster's mailing, the take buttons step aside for theirs; the card and the recipient list stay.
- Every part has its own switch in Wanderer's options; one checkbox gives you the game's window alone. /wmail says from anywhere whether a letter waits for you.
